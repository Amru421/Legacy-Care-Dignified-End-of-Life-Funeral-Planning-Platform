BALANCED, PROTOTYPE-BASED REVIEW · 1 MINUTE 42 SECONDS

LegacyCare experience feedback

A narrated first-impression review: what makes the planning experience approachable, where users may need more clarity, and what should be improved before real personal information is used.

LegacyCare — experience feedbackEnglish narration · On-screen captions

WHAT FEELS THOUGHTFUL

A gentle, manageable first impression

  • ♡
    Respectful tone. The calm visual style and reassuring language suit a sensitive subject.
  • ↗
    Clear starting point. The dashboard and separate sections make it easier to find wishes, people, and notes.
  • ✳
    Choice without pressure. People can record preferences, revisit their plan, and download a summary at their own pace.

WHAT SHOULD COME NEXT

Make trust match the caring design

  • 01
    Clarify privacy. Explain prominently what browser storage means and who can see saved data.
  • 02
    Test accessibility. Validate keyboard navigation, screen-reader support, contrast, and readability with older users.
  • 03
    Earn real-world trust. Verify providers and build secure, explicit-consent-based family access before real use.

Honest context: This feedback is a walkthrough of the published prototype, not a usability study with recruited participants. The demo has no accounts, encryption, verified providers, or authorized nominee access. Do not enter sensitive information.


# 🌿 LegacyCare – Dignified End-of-Life & Funeral Planning Platform

### Your Wishes. Your Dignity. Their Peace of Mind.

## 📌 Project Overview

LegacyCare is a web-based end-of-life and funeral planning platform designed to help individuals document their funeral preferences, last rites, cultural traditions, and personal wishes in advance.

The platform aims to reduce emotional stress and confusion for family members by providing a centralized space to record important instructions, nominate trusted contacts, explore funeral service providers, and organize relevant documents.

LegacyCare promotes dignity, transparency, privacy, and peace of mind through thoughtful digital planning.

## ❗ Problem Statement

End-of-life arrangements often create emotional and logistical challenges for families due to:

- Lack of advance funeral planning.
- Confusion regarding personal and religious preferences.
- Difficulty finding suitable funeral service providers.
- Unclear service costs and arrangements.
- Lack of centralized documents and instructions.
- Last-minute decision-making during difficult situations.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

LegacyCare provides a centralized digital platform where users can:

- Create and manage advance funeral plans.
- Record religious, cultural, and personal preferences.
- Select preferred funeral services.
- Add trusted family members or nominees.
- Upload and manage supporting documents.
- View and update their plans whenever required.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Enable advance funeral and last-rite planning.
- Preserve individual wishes and cultural preferences.
- Reduce emotional and logistical burdens on families.
- Improve transparency in funeral service pricing.
- Provide organized access to important instructions.
- Promote dignified and responsible end-of-life planning.

## 🔐 Privacy and Security

LegacyCare is designed around privacy-first planning.

The current prototype uses browser LocalStorage for demonstration purposes. It does not provide production-grade encryption, secure authentication, or remote nominee authorization.

A production implementation should include:

- Secure password hashing.
- Encrypted data storage.
- Role-based access control.
- Verified nominee authorization.
- Secure document management.
- Regular database backups.
- Audit logs and consent management.

**Important:** Do not upload real sensitive personal, identity, financial, legal, or medical documents to this prototype.

## ⚠️ Project Scope and Limitations

This project is currently a frontend prototype developed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Provider listings, prices, authentication, and admin functionality are demonstration features. No real bookings, provider communications, legal will drafting, or government document processing are performed.
